# Nutritual

Nutritual organizes dietary supplement intake routines and their reminders.

## Language

**Product**: A dietary supplement the user wants to include in their intake
routines.

**Product form**: The physical form in which a product is taken, such as powder,
liquid, or capsules.

**Intake routine**: A plan for taking a product on selected weekdays, every N
days from a chosen start date, or as needed. Scheduled times follow the user's
current time zone. An intake routine can be paused.
_Avoid_: Program, reminder

**Intake quantity**: The amount of a product intended for each intake in a
routine, expressed with a unit such as capsules, drops, milligrams, or millilitres.

**Intake**: A recorded occurrence of taking a product as part of an intake
routine, with the date and time it was recorded. Receiving a reminder does not
count as an intake.

**Paused routine**: An intake routine whose reminders are suspended while its
schedule is preserved. Resuming it uses the original schedule without catching
up on missed reminders.

**Skipped scheduled occurrence**: A scheduled intake occurrence the user
chooses not to take. It is kept separate from intake history and remains
skipped for that local day.

**Reminder**: A notification scheduled to signal an intake as part of an intake
routine. _Avoid_: Routine

**Paired phone**: The phone designated to receive intake routine requests from
the user's authorized ChatGPT connection. Only one phone is paired at a time.

**ChatGPT connection**: The user's authorization for ChatGPT to access and
manage intake routines on their paired phone. Revoking this authorization
preserves the phone's existing routines and intake history.
